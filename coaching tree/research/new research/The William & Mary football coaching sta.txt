The William & Mary football coaching staff (1969–1971) under Lou Holtz.

1969 Season
Head Coach: Lou Holtz (1st Season) Record: 3–7 (2–2 Southern Conference)

Name	Role	Start Year	End Year	Later HC?	HC School/Year	Confidence	Sources
Lou Holtz	Head Coach	1969	1971	Yes	NC State (1972–75), Arkansas (1977–83), Notre Dame (1986–96)	HIGH	[1, 2, 7]
Bobby Ross	Assistant Coach (Defense)	1967	1970	Yes	Maryland (1982–86), Georgia Tech (1987–91), Chargers (1992–96)	HIGH	[13, 16]
Larry Beightol	Offensive Line Coach	1968	1971	No	N/A	HIGH	[31, 35]
Bo Rein	Assistant Coach (Offense)	1969	1970	Yes	NC State (1976–79), LSU (1980)	HIGH	[33]
Chuck Clausen	Assistant Coach	1969	1970	No	N/A	HIGH	[31, 45]
Dick Harmison	Assistant Coach	1969	1972*	No	N/A	HIGH	[32, 44]
Notes:

Dick Harmison is listed as joining the staff in 1969. While some sources say he left "before 1973," sources confirm his hire in 1969.
Larry Beightol started in 1968 under Marv Levy as a defensive line coach but switched to offensive line under Holtz in 1969.
